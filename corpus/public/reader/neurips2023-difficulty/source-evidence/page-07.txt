To analyze the consistency/stability of the concept w.r.t data variations, we use small perturbations ϵ
to represent various inevitable variations in the data, such as shape deformation and object rotation
variations. Theorem 3 shows that high-order interactive concepts are much more unstable to inevitable
variations in the data than low-order interactive concepts. This makes high-order concepts more likely
to be influenced by data variations and less likely to be consistently present or absent in samples of
the same category, which boosts the learning difficulty.
