If the convolutional kernel size $K$ is small, then the decoder network is less likely to learn high-frequency components. It is because according to Theorem 4.2, a large $K$ value alleviates imbalance of the second-order moment $\operatorname{SOM}(\mathbb T^{(uv)(L:1)})$ between low frequencies and high frequencies caused by the imbalance of $|R_{uv}|$. Thus, a small $K$ value strengthens the trend of encoding low-frequency components of the input sample.
