The exponential increase of $\mathbb T^{(uv)(L:1)}$ along with the network depth $L$ indicated that the frequency component of the network output also increased exponentially along with $L$. Therefore, for the frequency component $\mathbf h^{(uv)}$ generated by the $l$-th layer in a real decoder network, we measured its second-order moment $SOM(\mathbf h^{(uv)})$. Figure 5 shows that $SOM(\mathbf h^{(uv)})$ increased along with the layer number in an exponential manner.

Besides, we visualized feature spectrums of different convolutional layers, which verified the claim that a deep decoder network strengthened the encoding of low-frequency components of the input sample. Results on the Broden dataset in Figure 4(a) show that magnitudes of low frequencies increased along with the network layer number. Results on more datasets in Appendix C.4 yielded similar conclusions.
