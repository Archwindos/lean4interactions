C       E XPERIMENTAL SETTINGS FOR VISUALIZING INTERACTION PRIMITIVES
C.1 M ODELS AND DATASETS
We used the models (including MLP, ResMLP, LeNet, AlexNet, VGG, PointNet, and PointNet++)
provided by Li & Zhang (2023b) and followed their experimental settings. The MLPs and ResMLPs
used in this experiment all had 5 fully-connected layers. Each hidden layer had 100 neurons. The
tic-tac-toe dataset, the wifi dataset, and the phishing dataset refer to the UCI tic-tac-toe endgame
dataset (Dua & Graff, 2017), the UCI wireless indoor localization dataset (Dua & Graff, 2017), and
the UCI phishing website prediction dataset (Dua & Graff, 2017), respectively. The MNIST-3 dataset
is a binary classification dataset, where images of the digit “three” in the MNIST dataset (LeCun
et al., 1998) were taken as positive samples, while images of other digits were taken as negative
samples. The CelebA-eyeglasses dataset is a binary classification dataset, where images with the
