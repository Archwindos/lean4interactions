E THIC STATEMENT
This paper aims to prove the emergence of sparse primitive inference patterns in well-trained DNNs
under certain conditions. The proof potentially provides theoretical support for explaining DNNs at
the concept level. There are no ethical issues with this paper.

R EPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT
We have provided proofs for the theoretical results of this study in Appendix B. We
have also provided experimental details in Appendix C.1, Appendix C.2, and Appendix
D.1. Furthermore, we have released the code at https://github.com/sjtu-xai-lab/
interaction-sparsity.
